# Communication Skills for Engineers

This subject focused on communication and people skills used in academic, professional and team environments.

## Areas Covered

### Professional and Career Communication
- Professional communication skills.
- Self-awareness and goal setting.
- Résumé preparation.
- Interview skills.
- Group discussions, their methods, simulations and common errors.
- Exploring career opportunities and building global competence.

### Personal and Professional Effectiveness
- Leadership and taking initiative.
- Stress and time management.
- Decision-making and problem-solving.
- Intercultural communication.

### Teamwork and Collaboration
- Cognitive and non-cognitive skills in teams.
- Presentation skills.
- Building trust and collaboration.
- Listening as a team skill.
- Brainstorming techniques.
- Social and cultural etiquette in teams.
- Conflict resolution.
- Internal communication and leadership within teams.

## Learning Focus

The subject covered how communication works in professional settings and how individuals contribute to a team. It also introduced the role of listening, clear presentations, collaboration and decision-making when working with people from different backgrounds.

These topics helped me understand that technical knowledge is only one part of working effectively in an engineering environment; communicating ideas and collaborating with others matter as well.
